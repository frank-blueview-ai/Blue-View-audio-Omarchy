All four lanes were detected by at least one mic channel at 1, 2, 4, 8, 12, and 16 kHz. At lower frequencies, the left-labeled lanes did not meet the confidence gate, while right-labeled lanes began meeting it at 160 Hz. This asymmetry may involve driver behavior, hardware mapping, microphone response, or placement/room effects. It does not establish a 1 kHz cutoff for the left speakers or justify boosting their bass.

No EQ, crossover, frequency limit, speaker route, or saved volume was changed. Application mutes were restored. Raw mic audio was discarded. Proper driver-specific calibration needs identified speaker topology and/or calibrated acoustic measurements.
